# Privacy Policy — Jellush: Block Match & Blast

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026  
**Developer:** DAGT Studio  
**Contact:** info@dagtstudio.com  
**Website:** https://dagtstudio.github.io/

## 1. Introduction

DAGT Studio ("we", "us", or "our") develops and operates Jellush: Block Match & Blast ("Jellush" or "the Game").

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency about how information is collected, used, stored, and shared.

This Privacy Policy explains our data practices, including the use of advertising, analytics, and crash reporting technologies.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on your use of the Game, applicable privacy choices, and enabled features, the following information may be processed:

**Gameplay and usage information**
- Levels started, completed, or failed
- Game sessions and session duration
- Moves, scores, stars, and gameplay progression
- Game phase transitions, combos, and booster usage
- General interactions with game features

**Device and technical information**
- Device model and operating system version
- Application version and language settings
- Application installation identifiers
- Advertising identifiers, where permitted
- IP address and approximate geographic information
- Diagnostic information, performance data, and crash reports

**Advertising information**
- Advertisement impressions and interactions
- Advertising performance and revenue events
- Rewarded advertisement completion
- Advertising consent and privacy preferences

**Purchase information**

If in-app purchases are available, purchase product identifiers, transaction status, purchase tokens, and entitlement information may be processed to validate purchases and provide purchased content.

We do not directly collect payment card details. Payments are handled by the applicable application store.

**Voluntarily provided information**

If you contact us or submit feedback, we may process your email address, message, and any information you choose to provide.

## 3. How We Use Information

We process information to:

- Provide and maintain the Game
- Save and manage gameplay progression
- Analyze gameplay difficulty and player engagement
- Improve gameplay balance and user experience
- Understand player retention and feature usage
- Display, measure, and manage advertisements
- Detect and resolve crashes and technical issues
- Prevent abuse and protect the integrity of our services
- Respond to support and privacy requests
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

We aim to minimize the personal information needed for these purposes.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Jellush uses or may use the following services in its published configurations:

**Google AdMob**

Used to display, manage, and measure advertisements. Google may process device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information according to applicable privacy requirements.

**Google Firebase Analytics**

Used to understand gameplay interactions, session activity, retention, and overall application performance.

**Google Firebase Crashlytics**

Used to detect crashes, analyze technical failures, and improve application stability.

These services may process information on infrastructure operated by Google or its authorized service providers.

For additional information:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google Advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- Firebase Privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 5. Advertising and Privacy Choices

Jellush may display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Where required by applicable law, we provide privacy and consent choices before processing information for relevant advertising purposes.

Depending on your location and choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited in accordance with applicable requirements.

Users may change applicable advertising privacy choices through available in-app privacy controls.

Advertising identifiers may also be managed through Android device settings.

We do not treat advertising consent as proof of a user's age.

## 6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Gameplay analytics help us identify difficult levels, performance problems, and opportunities to improve the Game.

Analytics and diagnostic collection are subject to applicable privacy requirements and the configuration of the relevant services.

Where consent or another privacy control is required, data collection is managed accordingly.

We aim to collect only information necessary for measuring gameplay and application performance.

## 7. Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, and certain gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.

Local progress data is not automatically equivalent to data collected by our online services.

Removing the Game or clearing its application data may delete locally stored progress, subject to operating system backup behavior.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for gameplay functionality, analytics, advertising operations, technical diagnostics, support, and applicable legal obligations.

Locally stored gameplay data generally remains until removed through available application or device controls.

Analytics and diagnostic data are subject to the retention settings and deletion capabilities of the relevant Google services.

You may request information about the processing or deletion of data associated with your use of Jellush by contacting:

**info@dagtstudio.com**

We will review requests and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and available technical identification and deletion capabilities.

Some aggregated or irreversibly de-identified information may no longer be attributable to an individual user.

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.

Google advertising and Firebase services use security measures for transmitted information, including encrypted network communication.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Jellush is intended for users aged 13 and older.

We do not intentionally design the Game to solicit personal information from children under 13.

Age-related privacy requirements may vary depending on the user's country or region.

Where applicable, additional protections are applied to users who are legally considered minors.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact info@dagtstudio.com.

## 11. International Data Processing

Information processed through our third-party service providers may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence.

Such processing is subject to applicable laws and the relevant service providers' data protection mechanisms.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Request access to information associated with you
- Request correction or deletion
- Withdraw applicable consent
- Object to or restrict certain processing activities
- Manage relevant advertising privacy preferences

To exercise applicable rights, contact:

**info@dagtstudio.com**

We may need additional information to identify the relevant data without collecting unnecessary personal information.
