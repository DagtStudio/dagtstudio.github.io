Common questions

How do I play?

Place the pieces on the board, then swap adjacent jellies to make a match of three or more. Check the level goal and the moves you have left before your next swap.

Where is my progress saved?

Jellush saves progress on your device. If progress looks different after changing phones or reinstalling, tell us what changed. Before troubleshooting, keep your current game installation so the save is available.

Why is an ad or a reward unavailable?

Ads depend on connectivity, availability and privacy settings. For a missing reward, tell us which reward you chose, whether the video finished and what happened when you returned to the game.

How do I manage privacy or request deletion?

Open the game settings to find your data preferences and any available advertising privacy options. To request deletion of data held by DAGT Studio, email us and describe your request. The privacy policy explains local saves and how advertising providers handle data.
